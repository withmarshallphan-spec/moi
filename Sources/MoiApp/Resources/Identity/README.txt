MOI Identity Pack

Included:
- moi_summon.wav: summon / assistant activation sound
- moi_notification.wav: ordinary Moi notification
- moi_success.wav: successful action completion
- moi_attention.wav: important but gentle attention cue
- moi_logo.png: current Moi logo

Sound direction:
Original, soft, airy, aurora-like, glassy tones designed to feel calm and recognizable.
They are not copies of Siri or any commercial notification sound.

Recommended use:
- Summon: when Moi expands from notch/top edge
- Notification: ordinary schedule/deadline banner
- Success: completed local action
- Attention: important transition or confirmation request

All WAVs are 48 kHz stereo PCM and can be bundled as macOS app resources.
